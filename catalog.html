Seaglass

A frosted-glass UI with its edges worn smooth by the waves. If Liquid Glass is a polished window, this is a shard picked up on the beach. Light passes through, but nothing reflects or refracts.

  • Surfaces are frosted glass. Only the background color blurs through. While dry, there are no highlights or reflections; the only glow is a faint brightening along the edge.
  • Corners are tumbled pebbles. Radii are slightly uneven, in point symmetry. Boxes that hold text (callouts, code) keep all four corners even, for readability.
  • Rarity is importance. White, grey and sea green, common on any beach, are for surfaces and dividers. Rare cobalt is for actions and selected states. Red, the rarest, is for danger only.
  • Touch makes it wet. On hover, glass gets a wet sheen and turns clearer; where there is no panel, a thin film of water appears. Pressed thumbs and checks turn clear like a lens.

Exchange rate

Accordion

details.accordion
How is rounding handled?
Fractions of a yen go to whoever paid that expense. You can switch to rounding up in settings.
Which rate is used for foreign currency?
The rate on the day the expense was recorded. You can also correct it by hand later.
Can I add members later?
Yes. They aren’t included in expenses from before they joined.
